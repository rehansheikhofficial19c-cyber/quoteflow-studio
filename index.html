FAQs

Questions, answered.

How long does a website take?

A typical target is 3–5 business days after we receive the required content, assets and approvals. The confirmed timeline will be included in your proposal.

What does the starter package cost?

Starter projects begin at $500 USD. We provide a written quotation after understanding your requirements.

Will my website work on mobile phones?

Yes. Responsive desktop and mobile layouts are included, and we check common screen sizes before handover.

Can you connect my domain and enquiry form?

Domain connection and form integration can be included once we confirm your hosting, domain access and appropriate form service. Third-party fees are discussed before setup.

Do I need to provide website text and photos?

We can help prepare website copy. You should provide accurate business details and brand assets. Photos must be supplied by you or sourced with appropriate usage rights.
