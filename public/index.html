Frequently asked questions

Good to know before we begin.

How long does a website take?

A typical target is 3–5 business days after we receive the required content, assets and approvals. Your confirmed timeline will be included in the proposal.

What does the starter package cost?

Starter projects begin at $500 USD. We provide a written quotation after understanding your requirements.

Will my website work on mobile phones?

Yes. Responsive desktop and mobile layouts are included, with common screen sizes checked before handover.

Can you connect my domain and enquiry form?

Domain connection and form integration can be included once hosting, domain access and the appropriate form service are confirmed. Any third-party fees are discussed first.

Do I need to provide text and photos?

We can help prepare website copy. You must provide accurate business details. Photos should be supplied by you or sourced with appropriate usage rights.
